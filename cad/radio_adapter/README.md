# 2004 Ford Focus SVT · JVC KW-V830BT Extended Radio Adapter

Custom 3D-printable Double-DIN mounting solution engineered for installing the **JVC KW-V830BT** into the **2004 Ford Focus SVT** center dashboard.

Built on the community baseline ([Printables Model 1216108](https://www.printables.com/model/1216108-ford-focus-mk1-radio-frame) by *kstor*) using an **Extended / Proud Forward Bezel Architecture** with a deep, rounded outer dome slope, a 3D Z-contoured console seating rim, and the **2-step (`188.50 mm` shelf + `183.00 mm` inner boss) side locator tabs** calibrated from physical test prints.

---

## 1. Key Fitment & Geometry Calibrations

1. **Widened Rear Base Flange (`196.00 mm` Top → `197.50 mm` Bottom) + Deeper Top-Corner Hug (`7.60 mm`):**
   - **Top Edge Base (`196.00 mm`, `X = ±98.00 mm` at `Y = +53.60 mm`, `R = 5.00 mm`):** Widened `+2.25 mm` per side with a tighter `5.0 mm` corner radius and `7.60 mm` top-corner sweep (`+2.0 mm` deeper toward the dash) to pull both upper corners flush and hide the upper dash cutout notches.
   - **Bottom Edge Base (`197.50 mm`, `X = ±98.75 mm` at `Y = -55.40 mm`, `R = 6.50 mm`):** Widened `+1.00 mm` per side along the lower console opening while cleanly clearing the driver-side ashtray/12V bezel and lower HVAC dials.
   - **Height strictly preserved at `109.00 mm`:** (`Y = -55.40 to +53.60 mm`) so it clears the hazard triangle above and HVAC dials below.

2. **Calibrated 2-Step Side Locator Shelf (`187.40 → 184.00 mm` Anti-Tilt Taper + `178.50 mm` Boss):**
   - **Step 1 — Tapered Console Opening Shelf (`187.40 mm` at collar → `184.00 mm` at rear):** Tapers linearly from front to back (+1.00 mm wider than 186.40 mm, +0.50 mm per side) to provide firm friction press-fit retention into the console opening. The lead-in edge enters easily at `184.00 mm` ($X = \pm 92.00\text{ mm}$), and wedges securely to `187.40 mm` ($X = \pm 93.70\text{ mm}$) along the dash lip, locking the top edge flush against the upper brow.
   - **Step 2 — Inner Lead-In Guide Boss (`178.50 mm` wide, `X = ±89.25 mm`):** Steps inward by `2.25 mm` per side (`4.00 mm` deep with chamfered ramps) to effortlessly center and guide the unit into the console pocket.

3. **Front Screen Inlay Lip (`1.00 mm` Thick):**
   - Recessed `1.00 mm` deep into the front face (`Z = 0.0` to `-1.00 mm`).
   - `5.00 mm` inlay on left and right (`169.70 mm` window width, `X = ±84.85 mm`).
   - Symmetrical `1.00 mm` inlay on top and bottom (`97.30 mm` window height, `Y = ±48.65 mm`).
   - Allows the bezel to sit completely flush with the JVC display face.

4. **Asymmetric Bottom-Right 3.0 mm Curve Extension (Matching Test Fit Photos):**
   - The Focus MK1 dashboard console exhibits a deeper sweep on the passenger-side lower corner near the cigarette lighter.
   - Added a smooth, $C^1$-continuous asymmetric contour term dropping an extra **`3.00 mm` deeper** towards the console at the bottom-right corner ($X = +98.75, Y = -55.40\text{ mm}$), completely eliminating the daylight gap visible in physical test-fit photos.
   - Bottom-left corner retains the original calibrated `5.00 mm` drop, while bottom-right drops `8.00 mm` relative to bottom center.

5. **Multi-Row Continuous ISO M5 Screw Slots:**
   - Continuous `54.0 mm` slots spanning `Z = -19.0 mm` to `Z = -73.0 mm` with zero bridge obstructions.
   - Lower row at `Y = -26.00 mm` (~22–26 mm from bottom of chassis).
   - Upper row at `Y = +19.00 mm` (exactly `45.0 mm` center-to-center above lower row).
   - Center row at `Y = -3.50 mm`.
   - Accommodates standard 4-hole grids, diagonal patterns, and any forward/rear depth position.

6. **Hidden Debossed `▲ TOP ▲` Orientation Label on Rear Rim:**
   - Located at the center of the rear seating rim ($X = 0, Y = +51.60\text{ mm}$).
   - Debossed `0.50 mm` deep into the rear surface with directional arrows pointing directly towards the top edge (`▲ TOP ▲`).
   - Completely invisible once installed into the dashboard (hidden against the dash opening), but makes part orientation instantly identifiable when holding the unit during assembly and installation.
