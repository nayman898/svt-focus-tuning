# Throttle-body bypass fitment

The [integrated flat-face revision](../iacv_flat_face/README.md) implements the photo-based feed route on the extended-neck V2.

The fitted Codex V2 flange has a nominal Ø65 mm main bore and a round Ø73–79.4 mm O-ring groove centered on the bore. The 79 × 79 mm bolt grid is the datum for tracing a bypass port because the bore is offset from that grid.

The second opening in the factory throttle-body reference photo is the idle-air control valve (IACV) feed. The planned route runs from that opening through a sealed flange passage to the isolated IACV supply pocket. The valve's existing outlet meters air into the plenum. The current CAD feeds the supply pocket through a side tap. A working flange feed would let the side tap be sealed when using that configuration. [Ford idle-air system description](https://www.fordservicecontent.dealerconnection.com/pubs/content/~WVX2/~MUS~LEN/15/VX21026.HTM)

## Capture the actual mating face

The [factory photo fit plate](factory_photo_plate/README.md) gives a first-pass opening and gasket trace using the 79 mm bolt pitch. Compare the pattern with the actual hardware before machining the seal or connecting the supply passage.

1. Print the [tracing sheet](bypass_capture_1to1.svg) at 100% and measure the 50 mm scale bar. Use the lower view for the back of the throttle body with the installed hood direction at the top.
2. Align the actual throttle body or gasket to the four bolt centers. Trace each bypass opening and the gasket or molded seal. Label the BBK and factory parts separately if both are being checked. A straight-on photo with a ruler in the same plane works too.
3. Mark the IACV feed on the trace and confirm where it meets the upstream side of the closed blade. The opening profile and seal boundary on the actual hardware determine the flange passage and gasket land. Capture the BBK separately if its feed differs from the factory part.

The capture drawing marks the current groove edges so a trace can reveal conflicts. The checks for the resulting flange are seal continuity, separation between supply and plenum through the valve, clearance from the fasteners and linkage, and wall thickness along the bypass route.
