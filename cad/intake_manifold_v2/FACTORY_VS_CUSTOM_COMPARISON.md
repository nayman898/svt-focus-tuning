## Calibration & Methodology Notes

- **Log Calibration (`v037` car logs, `2026-11-06`):**
  - Pull 5 (`10.22 psi @ 4,886 RPM`): `22.32 lb/min`
  - Pull 2 (`18.33 psi @ 5,978 RPM`): `35.91 lb/min`
  - Pull 6 Peak (`18.04 psi @ 6,397 RPM`): `37.85 lb/min`
- **OEM Baseline Basis:** OEM DSI curves are modeled from OEM geometry (`acoustic = 1.000` for OEM Short and Custom V2; matched pressure-loss recovery only).
- **Equal-Fuel AFR Note:** Projected `10.93–11.03` AFR band assumes equal per-cylinder fuel delivery and reflects modeled 1D VE distribution (`0.9828 / 0.9821 / 0.9811 / 0.9740`), not a measured knock-margin test.
