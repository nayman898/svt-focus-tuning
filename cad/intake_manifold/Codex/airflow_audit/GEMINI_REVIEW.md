# Airflow suite engineering review for Gemini

## Design decision

Use the current Stage-2 CAD as the fit baseline. Fix the airflow verification before choosing a new taper, scoop angle, runner size or throttle-body angle. The 2 mm result came from a sealed numerical domain with a mass source. The 4 mm result conserves the projected face flux, but the cylinder split still changes with iteration count. The best-supported geometry change to investigate is the sharp lip at cylinders 3 and 4, which is visible in the current exported STL. Design any internal blend together with the exterior pressure-wall geometry and compare it with the same baseline under verified test conditions.

The review covers `generate_intake_manifold.py` where it defines the fluid and interfaces, `verify_airflow.py` from extraction through reporting, and `compare_factory_manifold.py` including its telemetry, AFR and power calculations. It uses the current Stage-2 STL and both archived and newly generated results. The WRC verifier also uses the `0.95 × dx` outlet cutoff and warrants the same boundary test. ## 1. The fine-grid run selects no outlets

The spigot plane lies at `Y = −146 mm`. At 2 mm, the center-point rasterizer excludes the centers on that plane and the first occupied row is `Y = −144 mm` with **362 cells per runner**. The outlet predicate requires `Y ≤ −144.1 mm`, so the sole connected 365,581-cell domain gets **zero outlet faces** despite having **1,184 inlet-source cells**. The pressure matrices then lack their Dirichlet outlet anchors while the imposed net source violates the compatibility condition for an all-Neumann pressure problem. The initialization and correction CG solves hit their limits of 700 and 600 steps, respectively. After one outer iteration the maximum reported Mach was about **5,301**. Its zero loss, equal shares and 100% mass error are invalid outputs. At 4 mm the numerical cutoff is `Y ≤ −142.2 mm` and it selects **89 faces per runner**. [Voxelization and outlet selection](../../verify_airflow.py#L273) · [Pressure setup and solver status](../../verify_airflow.py#L394) · [2 mm reproduction](fvm_2mm.json)

Tag the four outlet patches geometrically from each CAD spigot cap and tag the inlet from the throttle-body cap. Use true boundary faces or cut-face intersections rather than a fraction-of-cell test on centers. Check the face counts, orientation, areas, disjoint masks and connectivity before solving. A run with imposed inflow needs an outlet on every sourced component. Return a failed run with the boundary diagnostics if any expected outlet is empty, and retain the iterative solver exit codes and residual norms. Test grid offsets as well as grid spacings. Expanding the old numerical cutoff to include one more row is a diagnosis of this case. A validated boundary definition must remain correct across grid phase, resolution and geometry. [2 mm outlet-row diagnostic](fvm_2mm_outlet_row_trial.json)

In the isolated outlet-row trial, the cutoff change restored 362 faces per runner and brought first-iteration maximum Mach down to **0.152**. CG still reached its iteration cap on initialization and on all four corrections. The fourth-iteration signed outlet flow was **0.277694 kg/s** with the public global mass error rounding to 0.00%. This confirms the boundary defect and shows why the rounded global number cannot serve as a convergence check. [Controlled outlet-row trial](fvm_2mm_outlet_row_trial.json)

## 2. The outlet reporting and 3D convergence need independent checks

- **Inactive runners inherit another runner's flux.** `om_lookup` starts at `-1` for non-outlet cells. The reporting loop indexes the projected outlet array with `-1` for every inactive runner, which reads the last active face. With only cylinder 2 open, the signed trace measured `[0, 0.27770, 0, 0] kg/s`, but the public result reported `[0.27819, 0.27770, 0.27819, 0.27819] kg/s` and **300.53%** mass imbalance. Intersect each cylinder mask with `all_outlet_mask` before reading flux. Report masked runners explicitly and mark the four-way spread unavailable for single-outlet cases. Test all-open, each individual runner open and invalid requests. Include signed net flow and reverse flow separately. The current positive clipping, default `25%` shares at zero total flow, and nonnegative ΔP clipping can hide a failed condition. [Outlet aggregation](../../verify_airflow.py#L584) · [Cylinder 2 probe](fvm_4mm.json)
- **Solving the pressure correction is only one convergence check.** At 4 mm, all 81 CG solves and all 240 BiCGSTAB solves returned success through 80 outer iterations. At 40 iterations, the projected local continuity defect was approximately `5.0e−9` of inlet flow, using summed absolute cell imbalance. The analogous flux reconstructed from the relaxed cell velocities was about **1.55 times** inlet flow by the same metric. That number is a sum of local absolute discrepancies. The signed projected outlet flow still totals **0.27770 kg/s**. The momentum operator constructs its upwind face transport from cell-velocity averages, so it does not reuse the continuity-corrected face flux used for reporting. Carry consistent signed face fluxes through the pressure and momentum steps, and track outer momentum and continuity residuals, face balance, pressure drop and cylinder shares. On the current geometry, 4 mm cylinder spread rose from **0.35% at 40** to **1.41% at 80**. The archived geometry showed **0.35% to 1.45%** in the prior rerun. [Momentum assembly and Rhie–Chow correction](../../verify_airflow.py#L489) · [Current iteration trace](fvm_4mm.json) · [Prior case](../airflow_reproduction.json) · [NASA on iterative convergence](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/wind/valid/tutorial/iterconv.html)
- **Pressure sampling changes the reported quantity.** The inlet is a volumetric momentum source in a plug of selected cells. The outlet is a zero-gauge sink behind a 24 mm porous region with a fixed `K = 14`. The reported `tb_to_plenum_dp_*` is a mean of cell-based total-pressure differences from the inlet plug to four pre-spigot sampling volumes. Use explicitly named, flux-weighted total-pressure sections outside the source and downstream-resistance zones, with geometric datums and reference planes in the output. For a compressed-air bench use its measured temperature, pressure, flow and outlet configuration. The solver's constant density, spatially constant eddy viscosity and empirical wall/port drag make it a steady screening model. Document these assumptions and benchmark the pressure drop before interpreting small design deltas. [Boundary conditions and loss sampling](../../verify_airflow.py#L394) · [NASA on spatial convergence](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/wind/valid/tutorial/spatconv.html)

## 3. The CAD data must follow the exported revision

- **The current throttle neck is a different air domain.** The new generator moves the plenum/neck transition, changes the neck taper and updates the IACV and exterior details. The current nominal bare plenum is **2.038 L**, versus **2.024 L** in the saved airflow report. At 4 mm the newer domain has **45,486 cells**, versus **45,540** in that report. The factory script reads the current parameters and manifest alongside the older `boost_optimized` record, so it can generate internally mixed results. Both report drivers executed. Both exports omit a comparison of CAD, parameter, solver and STL hashes. The new full rerun gives 2.447 kPa at 40 iterations versus 2.427 kPa in the saved report. The symmetric difference between the old and new main flow domains is 6.840 cm³. Put an input fingerprint and case ID on the JSON and plots. Gate the factory comparison on matching provenance, and label a legacy reconstruction as such. [Current parameters](../../intake_manifold_parameters.json) · [Generator](../../generate_intake_manifold.py#L302) · [Saved report composition](../../verify_airflow.py#L1244) · [Factory input reads](../../compare_factory_manifold.py#L297)
- **The extracted domain and geometry measurements need consistent stations and a solid cross-check.** The main domain is reconstructed from the plenum, TB and runner cutters. Vacuum ports, IACV passages, seam steps and as-printed deviations are outside that model. Use the assembled STL or production CAD cavity as the geometry authority for the run, record which accessory ports are sealed or active, and cross-check shared CAD cuts against the solid. Make separate WOT and idle-bypass cases. Slice each runner normal to its own centerline. A direct Stage-2 throat section measured **1,522.90 mm² and 138.63 mm perimeter**. The model uses a nominal rounded rectangle at **1,523.6 mm² and 139.04 mm**. The present area difference is small, but that formula can conceal changes in actual diameter, shape, perimeter or a local contraction. Measure the minimum and the profile along each runner, including through the boot and into the lower-manifold reference section if those pieces enter the test. [CAD extraction and section metrics](../../verify_airflow.py#L92) · [Section evidence](model_evidence.json)
- **The `0.00%` blockage is assigned by the flush branch.** It means there is no separately modeled inserted sleeve in that case. The envelope projection for the protruding sleeves is itself sampled and its plotted area is additionally reduced by a Gaussian wake assumption. For example, the cylinder 1 shadow changed from **13.85% to 13.63%** in a 0.5 mm sectional polygon-union check. Keep measured solid shadow, measured section area and an empirical wake-loss model as distinct quantities. The current STL still witnesses **0.67 mm** and **0.75 mm** spans through sharp internal lips on cylinders 3 and 4. These spans cross material between air spaces. The new IACV pocket floor measured **5.55 mm at center and 4.63 mm at the thinnest of 468 sampled vertical rays**, an improvement over the prior `3.41 mm` sample. Use the new geometry for that check and assess minimum pressure-wall thickness over the full candidate joint before accepting an internal blend. ## 4. The steady network, crank-cycle model and taper sweep answer different questions

- **The steady network imposes 98% VE and total flow.** At 7,000 RPM, it imposes approximately **0.27774 kg/s** and adjusts a common backpressure until the branches meet that sum. The reported per-cylinder VE is then normalized to the input mean. That supports a modeled distribution at a prescribed load. Absolute VE and airflow capacity need their own operating condition and validation. The current 3,000, 7,000 and 7,500 RPM Stage-2 cases bracketed the solve. At 7,500 RPM the high-flow end of the pressure bracket was only **0.30212 kg/s** against **0.29757 kg/s** demanded. Add bracket and convergence residual checks to avoid silent saturation at other conditions. Several taper, scoop, cant, friction and wake coefficients are empirical. Test each factor independently and sweep calibrated coefficient ranges before using small changes in balance to clock the scoops. [Steady network and bisection](../../verify_airflow.py#L704) · [Bracket history](model_evidence.json)
- **The 720° result is net valve flow in a lumped model.** At 7,000 RPM the existing last-cycle traces integrate to **0.24164 kg/s**, or **85.26%** of displacement at the reference density. The displayed mean of **98%** comes from scaling the relative cylinder flows. At 0.25° per step and six cycles the result was **0.24142 kg/s** and the rounded spread stayed **0.20%**. That checks time-step sensitivity for this model at this point. The model still resets cylinder pressure between events, uses constant-density orifices and guessed valve lift and Cd, and lacks an independently evolving runner pressure. In particular `p_port = p_pl − loss` makes the inertance acceleration proportional to `+loss`; the quadratic term reinforces the sign of the current even when described as resistance. Use a consistent runner momentum and valve mass/energy model with measured valve timing and Cd, record reverse flow and periodic-state residuals, and name the reported output net admitted mass. Use measured cylinder-resolved evidence before calling it trapped charge or engine VE. [Crank-cycle implementation](../../verify_airflow.py#L840) · [Step and cycle comparison](model_evidence.json) · [NASA compressible mass-flow reference](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/mflchk.html)
- **The taper sweep carries stale CAD data and changes multiple assumptions.** It changes the taper parameter but passes the parent case's area, blockage and volume audit into every trial. At the 0.94 Stage-2 point the reused volume is **2.038 L**, while regenerated CAD is **2.231 L** and the lumped frequency changes from **118.5 to 114.2 Hz**. The analytic taper is also non-monotonic when the 0.94 nose ratio exceeds the fixed 0.88869 split ratio. The 0.60 design is plotted separately from the sampled taper grid. The comparison baseline also changes spigot dimensions, bellmouth type, entry, cant and taper, so the comparison cannot isolate one feature. Regenerate the domain and audit for each geometry, classify feasible tapers by the intended shape constraints and compare one design parameter at a time before optimizing jointly. The analytic Helmholtz calculation uses a fixed **275 mm** acoustic length, while the factory comparison prints **243 mm** total length. Label the lengths by physical definition, document the assumed excitation order, and verify it against a measured pressure trace or an acoustic model. The lumped frequency alone cannot establish spool improvement. [Sweep implementation and plot](../../verify_airflow.py#L1215) · [Lumped acoustics](../../verify_airflow.py#L941) · [Regenerated taper cases](model_evidence.json)

## 5. The factory comparison has a reversed AFR relationship and an assumed airflow gain

- **Under equal fueling, more air means a higher AFR.** The script currently divides mean VE by each extreme and assigns the highest AFR to the lowest-flow cylinder. Use `AFR_i = 11.0 × VE_i / mean(VE)`. The same illustrative Stage-2 data then give approximately **[11.021, 11.010, 10.996, 10.973]** for cylinders 1–4, reversing which cylinder is leanest. The numerical range remains near 0.05 and is a model projection, so it cannot establish knock margin, spark or boost headroom. [Factory AFR calculation](../../compare_factory_manifold.py#L330) · [Direct and inverted calculations](model_evidence.json)
- **Most of the stated custom gain is encoded in the acoustic multiplier.** At 7,000 RPM, the published outputs are **41.30 lb/min custom** and **40.22 lb/min OEM short**. Holding the acoustic factor at the OEM-short value raises the custom pressure-loss-only estimate to **40.53 lb/min**. Roughly **0.77 of the 1.08 lb/min** claimed advantage is from the imposed custom acoustic multiplier. The asserted telemetry anchor also evaluates to **37.41 lb/min at 6,397 RPM** when re-run, versus **37.85 lb/min recorded**. The density scaling uses 72 °F while the run's pressure-loss calculation comes from the fixed 52 °C network. Treat those curves as scenarios until compared at matched conditions, use the exact logs and a common measured setup, and attach uncertainty to the OEM geometry and coefficients. Remove the headline power number until supported by a back-to-back test. ## Implementation and design handoff

1. **Repair and verify the suite first.** Add geometry and boundary tests on a straight duct and symmetric four-way manifold, including mesh translation and refinement, an empty-boundary rejection, four open outlets and every single-open case. Verify pressure drop against an analytical or measured duct benchmark. Output signed mass balance, each outlet area, outer and linear residual histories and flux-weighted pressure losses. Mark failed or incomplete runs before any chart or factory comparison consumes them.
2. **Rebaseline the current and reference manifolds.** Use three valid grid densities with comparable boundary conditions and converged results, quantify numerical uncertainty on pressure loss and cylinder share, and compare with repeatable bench measurements at matched flow, pressure and temperature. Use a flow matrix with all outlets open and individual or representative intake configurations. Use the existing **1.5% spread** target only with uncertainty small enough to distinguish a pass and to resolve the design deltas. Report the provisional steady and crank-cycle results with their own boundary conditions and definitions.
3. **Generate the CAD candidate after the baseline is defensible.** Try one local blend at the cylinder 3 and 4 internal junctions using section coordinates. Preserve the nominal boot bores, mating faces and sealing features unless the change calls for measured adjustments. Check shell thickness and IACV pocket isolation against the latest model, then re-export matching halves and rerun the same cases. Carry the tie-bar and other exterior changes through fit checks with the current geometry. A CFD case at 18 psig sets a flow condition. Pressure qualification of a real print is a separate assembly and materials test.
4. **Publish a traceable comparison.** Return the exact CAD, parameter and solver hashes, the design delta, the numeric uncertainty and a pass/fail result for each criterion. Keep OEM estimates, measured OEM results and custom predictions labeled with their evidence. Correct the report titles and tables at the same time. The present charts place imposed or normalized VE alongside 3D share and hard-code geometry labels, so a new plot with old labels can still mislead.
