# South Bend Stage 3 Endurance: Focus SVT

Research date:2026-10-30. Owner reports South Bend Stage3Endurance, an SBC
single-mass flywheel and stock IAC. The exact installed part number has not
been checked against an invoice or marking. Vehicle applications were untouched
while the owner reported the v018 write still underway.

## Best catalog match

**SBCSVT-SS-TZ** (older catalog spelling SBCSVT SS TZ) is the Focus SVT
Stage3Endurance kit. The [2013 DXD/South Bend application catalog, page17](https://blackclouddiesel.com/content/pdf/dxdcatalog5-17-13.pdf#page=17)
lists an included aluminum flywheel and425ft-lb torque rating. It labels the
application02–05SVT; that broad catalog year range is not independent proof of
vehicle production years or of the installed kit revision.

[South Bend's current product-family page](https://southbendclutch.com/products/sbc-clutch-kit-for-2002-2004-ford-focus-2-0l-diesel)
cross-references SBCSVT-SS-TZ with SD3-2636-45, Kevlar, Stage3 and a single-disc
configuration. It calls the usageSport rather than the historicalEndurance and
has a misleadingDiesel page title. Use the matching part number, not that title,
as evidence; verify current design/revision with the manufacturer if needed.

The old catalog lists24 in the product Weight column for the complete kit row.
It does not identify that figure as the bare flywheel's weight. Do not enter24lb
as flywheel mass. Audi/VW Stage3Endurance flywheel weights found in searches are
different applications and are not transferable to this SVT kit.

## What this gives Stage2A

An aluminum single-mass replacement is relevant to the idle torque-response and
driveline damping hypotheses. It does not establish a numerical reduction in
crankshaft-referred inertia or prove that the flywheel causes the warm dips.
The425ft-lb clutch rating is a torque-capacity specification, not inertia and not
a safe engine/gearbox power target.

No application-specific bare-flywheel mass or polar moment of inertia was verified
in the located primary material. Even a mass figure alone is insufficient:
rotational inertia depends on radial mass distribution, and the ECU coefficient
may represent more than the flywheel alone. Consequently the existing Engine
Inertia Torque0.0150 is not replaced with a guessed value.

To resolve that model, confirm the installed SBCSVT-SS-TZ revision/flywheel part
number and obtain bare-flywheel plus pressure-plate/disc inertia data (or measured
dimensions/mass distribution), along with the calibration parameter's units and
scope. A receipt/part marking is enough to confirm kit identity; no teardown is
requested solely for this research. No manufacturer message was sent.

Keep the planned rawMAF/fasterSTIT measurement and idle-air/spark investigation.
This lookup improves the hardware description; it does not justify changing
inertia, spark gain or dashpot by a percentage based on an unrelated kit weight.
