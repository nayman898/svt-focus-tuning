The existing P/N base-airflow curve is indexed by desired RPM: its documented
lookup around 1,100 RPM is about 0.516 lb/min, versus 0.460 at 1,000. Those are
lookup values before corrections, not measured total airflow or predicted valve
duty. No additional airflow reduction is applied. HP Tuners describes this
[desired-RPM airflow relationship and the retained startup/temperature adders](https://www.hptuners.eu/help/vcm_editor_parameters_ford_eng_idle.htm).

Changing the base target may also affect total desired RPM while retained adders
are active. This is not a startup-only adjustment, nor a repair for the recurring
startup O2 disagreement or reported 7.5-V cranking module supply. Startup and hot
restart behavior need comparison alongside the intended fully warm idle change.

state required by Editor, and stable write power. Those live write conditions
were not established by the saved-file comparison. The agent has not flashed
this candidate. The low cranking module reading still needs independent supply

Keep the first test stationary, in the same neutral/no-throttle scope and with
documented fuel/water-meth state. Use paired logging from before the start through
fully warm idle, one normal radiator-fan cycle plus 60 seconds after off, and the
same 30-second blower load when it can be distinguished from fan activity.
Compare actual/desired RPM, catch and restart behavior, mixture, trim, idle-air
duty, spark response and supply against v006. Earlier Scanner desired RPM read
1,088 with the 1,100 scalar; verify that channel's resolution before treating a
small numerical discrepancy as an idle-control error.

Accept this stage only if starting and load recovery remain satisfactory, with
no stalls or persistent hunting and no deterioration in measured mixture or
charging/oil-pressure behavior. No new physical pass/fail limits are invented
from this stationary log. If behavior worsens, stop the trial and use the exact
v006 rollback under appropriate write conditions. Keep v007 as a recorded trial;
do not proceed directly to 850 or combine further fuel/air/spark changes with it.
