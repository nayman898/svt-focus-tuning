# v004 closed-loop result and logging audit

## Result

The owner supplied v004 test logs and observed no closed-loop entry. The VCM
recording confirms **Closed Loop Active = No throughout**, **Closed Loop Ready
= Yes throughout**, **Fuel Source = Power Demand Enrich throughout**, and
**Closed Loop with O2Fault = OK throughout**. The low-RPM TPS-table experiment
did not produce the intended reported state change in this session. This does
not establish which remaining gate is responsible or prove a sensor is good.
Actual flashed binary identity remains owner/session-attributed, not read back.
No next calibration revision was prepared during this review.

## Inputs and timing

Both desktop exports and native recordings were copied without modification
to `logs/2026-10-26-v004-idle-01/VCM/` and `LW3/`, retaining the source files.
Copies were hash-checked and an input manifest saved with the session.

VCM has 19,805 exported rows, offsets 2.223-462.590 seconds: 460.367 seconds
of coverage. Coolant spans 154-196 F. Engine Run Time starts at 130 seconds,
reaches 255 at offset 126.922 and then remains 255. This channel is effectively
capped in the export; it cannot measure later runtime. The recording started
after engine start, but its elapsed coverage independently extends beyond the
retained 254-second startup gate. There is no basis to attribute the result
simply to stopping the recording too early.

There are 3,069 rows with coolant >=190 F and reported runtime >254, at offsets
367.066-438.497 seconds. They all retain the same four loop/fuel states above.
Rows are asynchronous/interpolated export observations, not independent samples.

The voltage/RPM disturbance remains relevant. Missing fan-state measurements
prevent positively identifying a fan event from the voltage dip alone.
The O2 channel contains varying data; that alone does not prove normal sensor
switching, heater health, or an accurate measured mixture.

LogWorks covers 473.088 seconds with 5,776 rows. Its displayed AFR spans
11.78-14.91, full-session mean 13.12; pressure spans -7.8 to -6.3 psi. RPM and
duty are zero throughout and are not established as usable signals. These are
whole-session statistics, not a synchronized warm-window comparison to VCM.

VCM creation field is 18:55:02 with no timezone. Two native LogWorks timestamp
candidates at byte offsets 81928/85172 decode to 18:54:49 UTC. If both describe
session origins on the same clock basis, the candidate mapping is
**LW elapsed = VCM offset +13 seconds**. Meaning, timezone, drift and latency
remain unvalidated; provenance was saved and no pointwise fuel corrections
were calculated. No prior session offset was reused.

## Next capture and diagnostic priority

Use the updated idle/cruise profile and record both applications from restart.
Before the long test, verify that the six added diagnostic channels actually
update. Inspect the missing B1 STFT, idle-air duty/STIT and raw TPS channels
during acquisition; if blank, preserve their unsupported/unavailable status
and inspect Scanner acquisition/channel diagnostics. Export all recorded
channels and verify a short saved sample rather than assuming a gauge proves
the recording is complete. Preserve DTCs and freeze-frame information before
any clearing; obtain the current diagnostic readout for the loop investigation.

Capture at least 6-8 minutes and fully warm coolant, noting fan and accessory
events. Use log elapsed time after the runtime channel caps. Obtain an actual
fan command/state channel if the controller exposes one that records; until
then manually note engagement. Continue separate wideband recording until its
wiring/scaling and integrated logging can be verified.

Next review should distinguish open-loop request/conditions, FMEM, and O2
fault/heater states before choosing another gate change. Review the actual
saved calibration's remaining enable conditions once these observations are
available. v004 is not accepted as a successful closed-loop fix. Do not expand
to driving/boost based on this result. If operation has deteriorated, stop the
test and use documented v003 rollback; fan circuit repair remains outstanding.
