## Limitations and competing explanations

- **No validated knock-retard channel.** Borderline Knock [19074] is a spark-related value, not measured knock retard. Earlier v035 documentation claiming zero knock, a proven MBT optimum, guaranteed power gains or complete mechanical protection is unsupported and must not be used for acceptance decisions.
- Fuel Pressure Sensor is raw volts; pressure transfer and sensor behavior remain unvalidated. Injector pulse width is labeled ms but exported at values such as 0.013852; units require verification before computing injector duty. Neither establishes fuel-system margin.
- Leaner measured lambda versus v034 is consistent with the fuel edit, but temperatures, boost and transient/sensor-delay differences remain confounders. No commanded lambda is available to distinguish enrichment logic from target tracking.
- Pull 2's warmup state, absent lambda and zero commanded duty corroborate the initialization problem. Its lower boost must not be interpreted as a tune result.
- Valid pulls have ECT roughly 186–190 F. Overall RPM minimum is 805.25 and maximum 6,710.5; no logged stall or RPM below 744 (target 944 minus 200). FMEM is No throughout. The final 20.441 s spans 870–1,203.75 RPM (mean 1,000.3), with minimum at 261.033 s, versus desired 944. This short, nonstationary-confirmed tail is not a settled-idle/fan-cycle validation. The drive continues after each pull, so no isolated post-pull idle recovery is established.
