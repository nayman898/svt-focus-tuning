## Comparison and next decision

The [earlier P1132 capture](2026-10-30-v015-p1132-review.md) had cold near-zero
O2 feedback, large trim reversals and fault fallback. Those conditions are absent
from this warm recording. However, that earlier sensor also switched once warm,
with warm-window RPM minima776–786 and lambda means near1.00. This is not a
controlled cold comparison and cannot prove the original sensor caused the fault.

The [3:40 sensor-check run](2026-10-30-v015-sensor-check-review.md) had a760RPM
minimum and blank state fields; its installed sensor identity remains unknown.
Do not label it an old-sensor baseline. The current run is more useful because
state fields populated and sensor identity is confirmed.

There is no evidence here requiring another immediate sensor purchase. A correct
application sensor remains the preferable permanent replacement if replacement
is indicated; warm switching alone cannot certify the15719's heater/pinout or
application equivalence. The small lean wideband average while trims are negative
is worth watching but does not justify a global fuel correction: sensor placement,
measurement bias and operating conditions have not been isolated.

Next comparison: after a full cool-down, log before starting the engine, on the
same tune and fuel pressure. Verify all state channels populate, keep accessories
unchanged, and capture initial O2 response, wideband, trims and FMEM/O2Fail flags.
Stop if running deteriorates; do not wait for a stall. Read current/pending DTCs
afterward without assuming O2FailNo means P1132 has cleared. No new DTC read,
code clearing, tune edit or ECU write was performed during this review.
