## What correlates

Across the three windows, mean IAC command falls 37.33 -> 36.66 -> 36.25%, mean
MAF 0.574 -> 0.561 -> 0.553 lb/min, raw MAF 0.595 -> 0.587 -> 0.582 V.
STIT becomes more negative (-0.188 -> -0.197 -> -0.202 lb/min), as does LTIT
(-0.034 -> -0.037 -> -0.039). These trends accompany increased variability;
they do not establish that adaptation itself is faulty.

Repeatedly an RPM peak has lower IAC and spark, then the dip brings increased
IAC and spark followed by recovery. At 32.664 s, RPM falls from about 985 half
a second earlier to 840; IAC rises 35.14 -> 41.15%, spark 6.89 -> 24 degrees.
STFT changes only -1.49 -> -2.03 percentage points. At 29.030 s, RPM falls from
938.5 a quarter-second earlier to 860 with STFT unchanged at -0.232%.
At 63.468 s, RPM falls from 982 to 860 over a quarter-second with STFT unchanged
at +0.879%. Thus a new STFT step is not necessary for every dip.

No switch to MBT or fallback appears: every exported row is Idle Feedback spark,
RPM Ctrl idle, Stoich fuel, CL Yes, FMEM No, O2 B1 Fail No. Desired RPM stays944.
Mean voltage is essentially unchanged (~13.75–13.76 V); there are brief lower
readings, but this is not evidence of a sustained electrical-load transition.
No fan-state channel is available here.

Fuel oscillation remains relevant. Mean STFT approaches zero (-3.48 -> -0.81 ->
+0.53%), while wideband variation increases. For 0–30, 30–60 and 60–end windows,
lambda mean is 1.012/1.007/1.002 and SD 0.020/0.033/0.033. Overall matched-window
range is 0.938–1.127. Near-stoichiometric averages do not prove stable combustion.
Asynchronous channel delivery and exhaust delay prevent identifying subsecond
fuel-versus-air causality from nearest timestamp pairing alone.

## Decision

This is a warm air/spark oscillation investigation with concurrent mixture
oscillation, distinct from the cold capture's sustained rich FMEM condition.
v019 has not eliminated the dips. Prior v018 warm SD30.77/min834.75 versus this
run's later SD~32.5/min839.75 does not demonstrate a meaningful improvement;
different conditions/windows prevent a controlled superiority claim.

Prioritize checking the warm base-air calculation, short/long-term idle-air
adaptation, IAC response, and relevant time/temperature transitions before another
MAF or spark-gain change. The observed decrease in commanded air makes this more
specific than simply repeating an unchanged idle log. Current data begins after
runtime232; the previously identified 165–200 s airflow-multiplier taper is not
captured and cannot be declared the cause. The first deep dip near displayed
runtime255 likewise does not prove a 255-second calibration trigger.

Do not alter multiple controls or disable closed loop based on this run. Preserve
v019 while auditing the applicable air tables and interpreting the runtime/ECT
channels. For any next trial, keep one continuous VCM capture through warmup so
the before/after180F interval survives export. Fuel-pressure hardware will add
physical evidence once installed and verified; current ECU pressure is not a
substitute for a physical measurement. No new calibration prepared in this review.
