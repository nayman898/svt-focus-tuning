## Causal Analysis & Motivation for `v026`

In `v005`, `ECM 32731` (`Open Loop Fuel TPS Thresh vs. RPM`) was modified at `0 RPM` and `1,500 RPM` from `8.00` to `200.00 counts` to prevent closed-throttle idle from tripping open loop on an absolute TPS reading of `~182 counts`. However, rows 3 and 4 (`2,000 RPM` and `2,500 RPM`) were left at `0.00 counts`.

Because `ECM 32731` has a threshold of `0.00 counts` at `>= 2,000 RPM`, any throttle application above closed throttle immediately trips Open Loop Power Demand Enrichment during stationary neutral holds and gentle light-throttle cruising. This bypasses closed-loop stoichiometric fuel feedback, commanding rich open-loop fueling (`Commanded Eq Ratio ~ 1.000` or richer) without load.

### Solution for `v026`:
Raising `ECM 32731` at **`2,000 RPM`** and **`2,500 RPM`** from `0.00` to **`350.00 counts`** creates a consistent, intentional threshold:
- Closed throttle (`~182 counts`) and light off-idle / cruise throttle (`< 350 counts`, or `~170 counts` / `~35%` relative pedal opening) retain Closed Loop stoichiometric control.
- Moderate-to-heavy throttle and boost (`>= 350 counts`) immediately transition into Open Loop Power Demand Enrichment.
- Preserves full protection for boost while eliminating unnecessary rich odor, smoke, and unmetered open-loop fueling in neutral and light cruise.
