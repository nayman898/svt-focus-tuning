## Causal Analysis: Throttle & RPM Oscillation during Stationary Holds

The user observed: *"6m 2k a lot better, oscillates around without too much throttle movement"*.

Detailed analysis of the high-resolution logs reveals why this occurs:
1. **Stationary No-Load Sensitivity**: In neutral with zero vehicle inertia, engine speed is extraordinarily sensitive to minute variations in airflow and torque. A throttle opening of only `252.8 ADC counts` (~1.23V, ~70 counts above idle) provides enough air for ~2,100 RPM.
2. **Spark Source Locking**: Throughout the holds, the ECU was using `Spark Source: Cold Start Emiss. Red.` (Cold Start Emissions Reduction), maintaining fixed ignition advance of `11.8°–12.0°` rather than using active spark torque control.
3. **Closed Loop Fuel Cycling**: As the narrowband O2 sensor swung rich/lean across stoichiometry (`STFT` moving between `+3%` and `+7%`), combustion efficiency varied slightly on each cycle. With fixed spark advance, fixed throttle plate, and ~48% IAC duty cycle, the engine speed naturally surged and tapered by `±60 RPM` around the hold target.
4. **Conclusion**: This oscillation is entirely normal for stationary neutral holds under fixed throttle and does not indicate a driveability defect. Under actual driving conditions (in gear with road load and vehicle inertia), vehicle speed dampens these minor cyclical variations.
